HOW IT WORKS

A simple start
to a lighter journey.

No account and no complicated dashboard. Send us your details through a straightforward quote form.

01

Tell us the route

Enter the pickup and delivery address. House/flat number and door number are required for both locations.

02

Describe your luggage

Choose the number of bags and add estimated weight, preferred dates and notes.

03

Submit your enquiry

Your request is saved for the BagBridge team. If the private WhatsApp notification is configured, the team can also receive an alert.

04

Confirm the details

The team follows up with available options. Wait for confirmation of price, service availability and terms before sending any luggage.
